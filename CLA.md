reviewed, finalized and enabled before contributions are accepted upstream.

the project name, not an assertion that a separate legal entity exists.

identified legal entity on whose behalf an authorized representative accepts.
"Contribution" means original code, documentation, tests or other material
that You intentionally submit to the Project for inclusion, and in which You
own or are authorized to grant the rights described here. Material clearly
marked as excluded from submission is not a Contribution. Merely referring
to, reviewing, or linking third-party material does not grant rights to it.

One acceptance covers Your intentionally submitted Contributions before and
after that acceptance, in the capacity identified in Your acceptance record.
No separate acceptance is required for each commit or pull request. This does
not authorize You to grant rights owned by an employer or another person.

grant the stated rights. A CLA does not erase a third party's license or

## 5. Transfer, acquisition and successors

You authorize the Steward to assign this Agreement and the rights and
obligations received under it to a person or legal entity acquiring or
assuming the Project, including through sale, acquisition, merger,
reorganization, inheritance or transfer of project ownership. **No renewed
contributor consent, signature or per-commit relicensing is required** for such
a transfer. The assignee must assume the Steward's obligations under this
Agreement in writing. Subsequent qualifying transfers are permitted on the
same terms. The grants continue for Contributions covered by this Agreement.

A future parent company can receive rights through such an assignment or a
separate sublicense. Approved partners can receive sublicenses under section
2 without becoming owners of Your copyright or parties to this Agreement.
An assignment or sublicense cannot convey more rights than the Steward holds.

Transfer records and acceptance evidence must be preserved with the Project.
The transferee's name and contact details may be updated in project records;
an ownership change alone does not require contributors to accept new terms.

## 7. Acceptance and records

Acceptance must identify the signer, the rights holder and capacity represented,
the exact agreement version and its SHA-256 digest, and the acceptance date.
An entity acceptance must identify its authorized representative. The record
must explicitly state agreement to the proprietary sublicensing and transfer
permissions, not merely certify code provenance or add a Git Signed-off-by.

The approved acceptance process will retain durable evidence of assent and
link it to a verified GitHub account. Private signature documents, legal names,
addresses and employer evidence must not be placed in public PR comments or
the public repository. Public automation receives only the account ID, scope,
version/digest, acceptance date and an opaque evidence reference. Contributors
must receive a privacy/retention notice before that process is activated.

A changed agreement must receive a new version and acceptance as required for
future submissions; it cannot silently rewrite an earlier acceptance. A
Project transfer complying with section 5 is not such a change.
